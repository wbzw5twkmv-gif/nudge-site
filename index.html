One small social challenge at a time.

Nudge is an iOS app for people who find social situations hard.

It hands you one concrete, doable challenge, like asking a stranger for the time or giving someone a genuine compliment. When you are done, you choose what kind of challenge comes next and whether it should be easier, the same, or harder. Nudge adjusts and shows you the next one straight away.

Progress shows as points, levels, and a tree that grows with you. The difficulty rises slowly and has hard limits, so the next step always feels small enough to actually take.
